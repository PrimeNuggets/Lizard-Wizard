Toon shader (Toon.shader, ToonLighting.hlsl, ToonLines.shader, Scripts/Editor/OutlineNormalsBaker.cs)

Written for our characters and props. These are the tutorials it's based on:

- Remapping the light dot product from -1..1 to 0..1 to drive the shading:
  michaelsgamelab, Unity URP Cel Shading Tutorial
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WQ0Gf1Ncjuw
  Their project is at https://github.com/michaelsgamelab/Cel-Shading but none of it was copied in.
  Instead of their step nodes we look the remapped value up in a painted ramp texture (Textures/Ramps).

- Half angle specular, banded additional lights:
  Unity, "Custom lighting in Shader Graph: Expanding your graphs in 2019" (character and concept by Minions Art)
  https://unity.com/blog/engine-platform/custom-lighting-in-shader-graph-expanding-your-graphs-in-2019

- Fresnel rim on the lit side:
  Daniel Ilett, Shader Graph Basics part 7 (custom lighting)
  https://danielilett.com/2024-05-07-tut7-11-intro-to-shader-graph-part-7/

- Inverted hull outline and storing smoothed normals for it:
  RVFX (FX Nari), URP outline shader video
  We bake the smoothed normals on import in Unity instead of Houdini, and store them in tangent
  space in uv3 instead of vertex colors so they work on skinned meshes.

- Screen space lines from depth + normals with a renderer feature (ToonLines):
  Shader Graph outline video using a Sobel filter on depth and normals
  We use the built in Full Screen Pass feature and a small HLSL shader instead of a custom feature,
  and the lines are a darker version of the surface color instead of solid black. Also picks up
  dents (nostrils, cracks) and fades out with distance.

- One shared lighting file for every asset, split into diffuse/specular/rim/ambient:
  Unite Copenhagen 2019, Learn how to do stylized shading with Shader Graph
  https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/learn-how-to-do-stylized-shading-with-shader-graph-unite-copenhagen-2019/179584810
  Unity, Customize the Lighting Model With Shader Graph's New Sample Content
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4v2fgeJa8z4

Usage: make a material with LizardWizard/Toon, it picks up ToonRamp_Warm by default. Paint a new ramp
(shadow on the left, lit on the right) to change how a material shades. Outline Width 0 turns the outline off.
Models in Art/ get smoothed outline normals automatically when they import.
Props are Cull Off with no hull outline for now (some prop meshes have flipped faces), ToonLines draws their lines.
ToonLines settings are on Materials/ToonLines.mat, the pass is on Settings/PC_Renderer.
