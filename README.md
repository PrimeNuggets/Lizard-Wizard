# Lizard Wizard

A 3D puzzle-platformer / rescue game developed as our capstone project.

Lizard Wizard is a very smooth balance of relaxing sliding and momentum-based movement. A whimsical 3D puzzle-platformer where you build speed across the dunes, carry your inertia through jumps and obstacles, choose your route carefully, and deliver water to thirsty critters before you run out. Or they perish of thirst.
